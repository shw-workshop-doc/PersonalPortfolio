My Projects

Hungry Hub

Food Recipe and Sharing Application

Hungry Hub is a food-based social networking application developed by a team of four members. It helps users discover recipes based on the ingredients available at home. For example, users can enter ingredients such as onion, chilli and salt, and the application suggests recipes that can be prepared using them. Users can also explore chefs, share pictures of their food, and interact with other users through likes and dislikes.

Key Features

  • Ingredient-based recipe suggestions
  • Discover and refer to chefs
  • Share food pictures and posts
  • Like and dislike other posts
  • Automatic deletion of posts receiving more than 10 dislikes
